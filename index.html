Svensa ("we," "our," or "us") operates the HIMS Mobile application. We respect your privacy and are fully committed to protecting your sensitive medical, personal, and device data. This Privacy Policy details how our platform collects, processes, transfers, stores, and handles information when you use our Digital Health Record system.

By using the application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect and Receive

To provide secure healthcare management functionalities and ensure seamless operational workflows, our app processes the following categories of data:

  • Personal Identification Data: Name, email address, contact details, date of birth, and official medical identity numbers or hospital identifiers captured during initial onboarding.
  • Protected Health Information (PHI): Laboratory test outcomes, clinician clinical documentation, medical history logs, diagnostic records, active prescription profiles, and demographic charts uploaded directly by you or synced securely from integrated healthcare facility systems.
  • Device & Technical Information: Cryptographic security tokens, operating system version profiles, unique device identification metrics, and specialized crash logs needed to maintain safe himsmobile:// secure protocol deep-linking structures.

🛡️ Critical Data Commitment: Your protected health information is heavily isolated. We do not sell, broker, or rent your healthcare logs to any third-party marketing entities, insurance carriers, or commercial brokers under any circumstances.

2. How We Use Your Data

The information we gather is exclusively leveraged to sustain and improve your internal application experience. We use this data to:

  • Populate and organize your personal Digital Health Record (DHR) interface dashboard.
  • Verify your user identity and authenticate access requests using secure cryptographic tokens.
  • Deliver mandatory system configurations, security updates, and critical operational alerts.
  • Comply with prevailing medical regulatory obligations, statutory protocols, and automated quality assurances.

3. Data Transmission, Storage, and Security

Because healthcare data requires the highest tier of security, we enforce stringent protection frameworks at every step:

  • Encryption In Transit: All digital communications between your mobile application instance and our background cloud infrastructure are completely secured using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) protocols.
  • Encryption At Rest: Any data files written to local mobile sandbox storage use native hardware-backed AES-256 cryptographic standards.
  • Access Control: System architecture uses zero-trust principles to ensure only authenticated users can access their corresponding clinical profiles.

4. User Rights and Data Deletion

You maintain absolute ownership and control over your recorded data profiles. You have the right to inspect, edit, or remove your data at any time:

  • Account Deletion: You can initiate a full erasure request directly within the settings dashboard of the app.
  • Manual Purge Requests: Alternatively, you may submit a formal deletion request through the application interface. Upon confirmation, all personal identity ties and medical history rows will be permanently scrubbed from our systems within 30 days, except where retention is legally mandated by regional healthcare compliance acts.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any modifications.
